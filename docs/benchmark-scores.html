Core / end-to-end success

One score. Full context.

A score belongs to a model, provider, harness, and benchmark contract. Not to a model name alone.

Score = 100 × average scenario success rate. Each scenario has equal weight. All recorded attempts count, including unavailable attempts. Only complete coverage of the pinned contract gets a headline score. This differs from Evidence's conditional repair rate, which excludes unavailable trials. Small samples are not definitive rankings; completeness is not statistical certainty. Speed and cost are not blended into the score.

Sort within each provider. Incomplete or unknown costs sort last; partial costs are lower bounds.
